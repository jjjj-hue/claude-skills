**Rules carried over from CLAUDE.md:**
- TRPA units → tenant-rep leads only. Never into the general OP pool.
- OP units → general leads, no-fee framing.
- Gross rent always. Concessions noted inline, never shown as net-effective price.
- MANO buildings move fast — check status before sending, don't let a stale entry go out.

**Status key:** 🟢 Ready to send · 🟡 Confirm before sending (app pending / price conflict / unconfirmed source) · 🔴 Hold / do not send
