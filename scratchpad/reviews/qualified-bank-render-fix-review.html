Concept callouts

Why a worktree branch goes silent git worktree add checks a path out onto its own branch by construction — that's the whole point, isolation. But it means anything committed there is invisible to main (and to anything that reads from main, like a republish step) until someone explicitly merges it back. No error, no warning — the commit just exists on a branch nobody's looking at.
Why ]const is fatal, not just ugly JS inserts a semicolon automatically at a line break when the next token can't otherwise continue the statement. With ]const on the same line, there's no break to trigger that rule, so the parser reads it as one malformed statement and throws immediately — before any of your code, including the render call, ever runs. A missing semicolon usually doesn't matter in JS; missing and no newline is the one combination that does.
